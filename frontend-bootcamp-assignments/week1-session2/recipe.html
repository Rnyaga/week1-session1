Chapati Recipe

Making soft chapati using simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups wheat flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add warm water gradually and knead into a soft dough.
  3. Add cooking oil and knead until smooth.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into equal portions and roll them out.
  6. Cook each chapati in a hot pan until both sides are golden brown.
